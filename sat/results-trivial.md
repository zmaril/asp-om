# SAT arm — phase 1 results (trivial cases)

Generated by `python3 sat/bench.py`. Machine: this repo's dev container; python-sat 1.9.dev5 (Cadical195, Glucose42), kissat 4.0.4 (external binary, DIMACS dump; its solve time is subprocess wall clock incl. DIMACS parsing).

Modes: **fixed** pins the layout to the clingo arm's layout via unit assumptions on the layout variables (same CNF generator); **free** lets the solver choose arm base/length/initial orientation and (case b) glyph placement from the radius-2 hex ball. Horizon T is iterated upward from 1 until SAT, so the first SAT row per series is the minimum makespan. Every SAT model was decoded and accepted by the independent forward simulator (sat/validate.py).

## Instruction-count optimum at the clingo arm's t_max

Objective identical to clingo's `#minimize`: number of non-wait instructions at fixed t_max (10 for case a, 16 for case b), minimized by downward linear search over `CardEnc.atmost` bounds with Cadical195. Times are total wall clock over the whole search (all solves + encodes) to the PROVEN optimum.

| case | mode | t_max | optimum (non-wait instr) | total time s | clingo (fixed layout) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

| a | free | 10 | 3 | 0.036 | 5 in 0.007 s |
| b | fixed | 16 | 12 | 0.234 | 12 in 0.120 s |
| b | free | 16 | 12 | 0.338 | 12 in 0.120 s |

## Validation & omsim note

* Every SAT result in the tables above was decoded (sat/decode.py) and re-simulated by the independent validator (sat/validate.py), which shares no logic with the encoder; all passed (goal reached, no hex collisions, legal grabs/drops, gripper on-board, bond semantics respected).
* omsim cross-check: NOT meaningful for cases (a) and (b) — they are synthetic instances of the shared phase-1 fragment, not real game puzzles, so no .puzzle file exists for them (the brief notes omsim is only useful for test case (c), Stabilized Water, which is out of scope for this phase). Rather than force a bogus .solution export we state this explicitly here; omsim validation is deferred to the Stabilized Water phase.
